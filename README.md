# jet-force-gemini-dual-stick

## Description
Jet Force Gemini Dual-Stick (DS) is a romhack for Jet Force Gemini aimed at quality of life improvements and adding a modern dual stick control scheme.

## Features
- Modern controller dual stick support with a new control scheme and sensitivity options in the 'Options' -> 'Controls' menu; left stick moves the player, right stick controls aiming (be sure to map your right stick to the c-buttons in Retroarch)
- More coming soon!

## Controls
- A, jump or jetpack boost (hold) if jetpack is unlocked and fueled
- B, crouch/crawl or jetpack hover (press while airborne) if jetpack is unlocked and fueled
- R, enter or exit aiming mode
- Left Stick/Joystick, player movement
- Right Stick/C-Buttons, reticle and aim movement
- D-Pad Left/Right, cycle weapons

## Compatibility
JFG-DS is developed on Windows and tested by a solo-dev (me, [TLRomhacks](https://github.com/TLRomHacks)). I exclusively test with Retroarch using the Mupen64Plus-Next core in mind. You are welcome to use other emulators, but I have not tested others and likely will not have the time to do so.

## How To Patch
This romhack comes in the form of a .bps patch. You must provide your own legally obtained rom of Jet Force Gemini (US). You may use any patching software/website to apply the .bps patch to your rom; I personally use [Hack64 Rom Patcher](https://www.hack64.net/tools/patcher.php).
